## About

Hostility Staff adds a single staff that turns mobs against each other — no summons, no mind control, just pure hostility.

Mark your targets, choose your sides, and watch the fight begin.

## Features

- **Duel** — choose two mobs and make them fight.
- **Gang up** — select a group of mobs, then choose a target. They will attack it together.
- **Brawl** — place a magic circle. Every mob inside is forced into a chaotic fight until only one remains.
